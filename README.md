 Hospital Emergency Room Dashboard

Project Overview:

The **Hospital Emergency Room Dashboard** is an interactive Power BI data analytics project designed to analyze hospital emergency room operations and patient information.

The dashboard provides insights into **patient admissions, waiting time, patient satisfaction, referrals, demographics, department referrals, and patient arrival patterns**.

This project demonstrates how **Power BI, data cleaning, data modeling, DAX, and interactive visualizations** can be used to transform raw hospital data into meaningful business insights.

 Project Objectives:

The main objectives of this project are to:

* Analyze the number of patients visiting the Emergency Room.
* Monitor the average patient waiting time.
* Measure patient satisfaction.
* Analyze patient admission status.
* Identify patient demographic patterns.
* Analyze referrals to different hospital departments.
* Understand patient visits by age group and gender.
* Analyze patient arrivals by day and hour.
* Track the percentage of patients seen within 30 minutes.
* Provide an interactive monthly and yearly view of Emergency Room performance.

 Dataset:

The project uses a hospital Emergency Room dataset containing information about patients and their ER visits.

 Important fields include:

* Patient ID
* Patient Age
* Age Group
* Gender
* Race
* Admission Status
* Patient Administration Time
* Admission Hour
* Department Referral
* Patient Satisfaction Score
* Wait Time
* Day Name
* Week Day
* Month Name
* Year

The dataset was cleaned and transformed before creating the dashboard.

 Data Preparation:

The following data preparation steps were performed using **Power Query**:

1. Imported the hospital ER dataset into Power BI.
2. Checked column names and data types.
3. Removed unnecessary columns where required.
4. Handled blank and null values.
5. Standardized categorical values.
6. Created useful date and time-related fields.
7. Created **Age Groups** for demographic analysis.
8. Created **Admission Hour** for hourly analysis.
9. Created **Month Name**, **Year**, and **Week Day** fields.
10. Validated the cleaned dataset before building visualizations.

Key KPIs:

The dashboard displays the following major Key Performance Indicators:

Total Patients:

Shows the total number of patients visiting the Emergency Room.

Average Wait Time:

Measures the average time patients wait before receiving service.

Patient Satisfaction Score:

Displays the average patient satisfaction score.

Patients Referred:

Shows the number of patients referred to other hospital departments.

Patients Seen Within 30 Minutes:

Measures how many patients were attended to within the target waiting time.

 Dashboard Visualizations

 1. Patient Admission Status

A comparison of:

* Admitted patients
* Not admitted patients
* Percentage of total patients

This helps understand the overall admission pattern.

 2. Patients by Age Group

Shows the distribution of Emergency Room patients across different age groups.

 3. Patients Seen Within 30 Minutes

A donut chart compares:

* Patients who met the 30-minute target
* Patients who missed the target

This helps evaluate Emergency Room service efficiency.

 4. Patients by Gender

Displays the distribution of patients by gender.

 5. Patients by Day and Hour

A combination of charts and a heatmap shows when patient visits are highest.

This helps identify:

* Busy days
* Peak hours
* Low-traffic periods

 6. Patients by Department Referral

Shows the number of patients referred to different departments, such as:

* General Practice
* Orthopedics
* Physiotherapy
* Cardiology
* Gastroenterology
* Neurology
* Renal

 7. Patients by Race

Displays the distribution of patients across different racial groups.

 8. Monthly Trend Analysis

The dashboard includes interactive **Year** and **Month Name** filters to analyze Emergency Room performance over time.

 Key Insights from the Dashboard

Based on the displayed dashboard:

* The selected period contains **431 patients**.
* The average patient waiting time is approximately **36.7 minutes**.
* The average patient satisfaction score is **4.72**.
* **179 patients** were referred to other departments.
* **283 patients (65.66%)** were seen within 30 minutes.
* **148 patients (34.34%)** were not seen within the 30-minute target.
* The admission analysis shows **224 admitted patients** and **207 non-admitted patients**.
* Patient visits vary considerably by age group.
* The dashboard highlights differences in patient volume across days and hours.
* General Practice has the highest number of department referrals among the displayed referral categories.

> **Note:** The above values are based on the dashboard's currently selected filters. Changing the Year or Month filters will change the results.
